Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS) Threshold Regulations

  • Inflammable Gas (Methane CH4): Regulation 169 mandates immediate withdrawal of all personnel if percentage exceeds 1.25% in general body of air.
  • Noxious Gas (Carbon Monoxide CO): Regulation 153 sets maximum permissible 8-hour exposure limit to 50 ppm (0.005%). Exposure above 200 ppm causes acute asphyxiation.
  • Air Velocity & Wet-Bulb Temperature: Environmental limit in working places shall not exceed 33.5°C wet-bulb without high-velocity ventilation.
THE STATIC SIX — HARDWARE SPECIFICATION & PINOUT WIRING
1. BRAIN 1 (ESP32 ROBOT MAIN CONTROLLER)
  • TF-Luna LiDAR: UART (TX: GPIO17, RX: GPIO16) or I2C. Single-point laser distance measurement.
  • Servo Mirror Sweep: PWM Signal GPIO18. Sweeps mirror 0° to 180° for planar radar scan.
  • Sensors: MQ-4 (ADC GPIO34), MQ-7 (ADC GPIO35), BME280 (I2C SDA: GPIO21, SCL: GPIO22), IR Obstacle (GPIO19).
  • LoRa SX1278 (RA-02 433MHz): SPI (SCK: GPIO18, MISO: GPIO19, MOSI: GPIO23, NSS: GPIO5, DIO0: GPIO2).
  • Camera Trigger Pulse: GPIO4 output pulse sent to ESP32-CAM when soil scoop cycle completes.
2. BRAIN 2 (ESP8266 BASE STATION BRIDGE)
  • LoRa RX: Receives telemetry packets from Brain 1 over 433MHz frequency.
  • Transport: Outputs newline-delimited JSON over USB Serial (115200 Baud) directly to laptop dashboard Web Serial API.
3. CAMERA UNIT (ESP32-CAM AI-THINKER OV2640)
  • Endpoints: Direct HTTP WiFi link: GET http://<ip>:81/stream (MJPEG) and GET http://<ip>/capture (JPEG snapshot).
  • Trigger: Captures and stores soil/water sample photos upon GPIO trigger from Brain 1.
